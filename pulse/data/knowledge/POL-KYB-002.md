KNOW YOUR BUSINESS AND ENTITY VERIFICATION STANDARD

1. Purpose and scope
This standard applies to every legal entity applying for acquiring, payment or credit services, in
every region in which the firm operates. It sets the minimum verification evidence required before
a merchant may be boarded and the treatment of discrepancies found during verification.

2. Minimum verification set
For each applicant entity the firm must verify, from an independent source: registered legal name,
registration number, registered address, incorporation date, current registry status, and the
identity of directors and equivalent officers. Verification must be recorded with the source,
retrieval date and confidence. Applicant-supplied data alone is never sufficient evidence.

3. Registry status
An applicant whose registry status is not active must not be boarded. Dissolved, struck off,
liquidated, in administration and equivalent statuses are declines, not referrals. Where the
registry status changes after boarding, the merchant must be restricted and reviewed within one
business day.

4. Treatment of discrepancies
Where applicant-supplied information conflicts with the register, the case must be escalated for a
step-up review. A discrepancy in legal name, director identity or registry status is high severity
and requires documentary resolution before boarding. A discrepancy in trading address alone is
medium severity and may be resolved by a utility bill, lease or bank statement no more than three
months old. Discrepancies must never be silently overwritten with the registry value; both values
and their sources must be retained.

5. Beneficial ownership
Beneficial ownership must be established to natural persons holding 25% or more of the entity,
directly or indirectly. Ownership must be traced through intermediate holding companies to at least
three levels. Where 25% or more of the ownership of an applicant cannot be established, the
application must be referred to financial crime for enhanced due diligence and may not be
auto-approved.

6. Refresh
Verified attributes expire. KYB evidence older than the review cadence for the merchant's risk band
must be refreshed: twelve months for high risk, twenty-four months for medium risk, thirty-six
months for low risk, and immediately on any material change event such as a change of control,
change of registered address, change of business model, or a new adverse screening result.

7. Records
All verification evidence, including negative results and disagreements, must be retained for a
minimum of five years after the end of the relationship and must be exportable as a single pack on
request from a regulator or scheme.
