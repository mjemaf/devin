CHARGEBACK AND DISPUTE RISK MANAGEMENT POLICY

1. Purpose
This policy defines the firm's tolerance for dispute activity across the merchant portfolio, the
interventions available, and the escalation path when scheme monitoring thresholds are approached.

2. Thresholds
The firm monitors the monthly chargeback-to-transaction ratio for every merchant. A ratio at or above
0.9% places the merchant in scope of scheme monitoring programmes and requires restriction, a
remediation plan and monthly review. A ratio at or above 1.5% requires a rolling reserve, a volume
cap, and consideration of termination. Ratios must be assessed on both count and value, and on a
rolling three-month basis as well as the current month.

3. Interventions
Available interventions, in ascending order of customer impact: enhanced monitoring; requesting a
remediation plan; requiring delivery-evidence or 3-D Secure enforcement; a volume cap; delayed
settlement; a rolling reserve; suspension of settlement; and termination with notice. The least
intrusive effective intervention must be selected and the rationale recorded.

4. Root cause
Dispute activity must be classified by root cause — fraud, non-delivery, quality, subscription and
free-trial practices, or processing error — because the correct intervention differs by cause. A
merchant with rising non-delivery disputes and lengthening delivery times is a potential insolvency
signal and must be assessed for credit exposure, not only for dispute cost.

5. Reserves
Reserves are held against future dispute and refund exposure, sized to expected exposure over the
dispute window, and must be reviewed quarterly. A reserve increase reduces available funds to the
merchant and is treated as a customer-detriment action requiring human approval.

6. Termination
Termination for excessive disputes requires: documented breach of threshold over at least two
consecutive months, evidence that lesser interventions were applied or were inappropriate, notice in
accordance with the merchant agreement, and approval by the risk committee delegate. Terminated
parties, including their directors and beneficial owners, must be added to the internal negative
file with the reason and date.
