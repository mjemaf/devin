PERPETUAL MONITORING AND PERIODIC REVIEW STANDARD

1. Principle
Risk is assessed continuously, not annually. A periodic review calendar is a backstop, not the
control. Every material change event must trigger reassessment within the timeframe set out below.

2. Trigger events
The following are material change events requiring reassessment: any watchlist or sanctions list
update affecting the screening population; a registry status, officer or ownership change; a change of
control; a change of settlement bank account; a change of website content or business model; volume
at or above three times the underwritten expectation; a dispute ratio at or above scheme thresholds;
adverse media on the entity or its owners; the off-boarding of a connected party; and any new link
discovered to an entity previously off-boarded for cause.

3. Timeframes
Sanctions list updates must be actioned within one business day. Registry status changes and connected
party off-boarding within two business days. Business model drift, volume and dispute triggers within
five business days. Adverse media within five business days.

4. Business model drift
Drift is a difference between what the merchant currently sells and what was underwritten. It is
detected from merchant category code changes, website content changes, transaction descriptor changes,
basket composition and dispute reason mix. Drift into a restricted or prohibited category is an
immediate restriction, not a review item.

5. Periodic review cadence
High risk merchants must be reviewed at least every twelve months, medium risk every twenty-four
months, and low risk every thirty-six months. An overdue review is a control breach and must be
reported in the monthly compliance pack.

6. Network monitoring
Relationship data must be re-evaluated on every change to ownership, officers, addresses, settlement
accounts and devices. Where a merchant is found to be connected to a party off-boarded for cause, the
merchant must be reviewed irrespective of its own performance, and the aggregate exposure of the
connected group must be assessed as a single exposure.

7. Evidence
Each reassessment must record the trigger, the evidence retrieved, the decision, the reason codes and
the reviewer. A reassessment that results in no action must still be recorded.
