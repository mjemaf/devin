SANCTIONS AND WATCHLIST SCREENING POLICY

1. Obligation
The firm must not establish or maintain a relationship with, or process a payment for, any person or
entity subject to financial sanctions in any jurisdiction in which it operates. This obligation is
absolute and is not subject to risk appetite, commercial override or materiality thresholds.

2. Screening population
Screening must cover the applicant entity, its trading names, its directors and equivalent officers,
its beneficial owners at 25% or more, and any authorised signatory on the settlement account.
Screening lists must include applicable consolidated sanctions lists, politically exposed person
data, internal watchlists and the internal negative file of parties previously off-boarded for
cause.

3. Timing
Screening must be performed before boarding, on every change to the screening population, on every
list update, and at minimum daily for the active book. Screening at boarding only is not compliant
with this policy.

4. Match handling
A confirmed sanctions match on the entity or any beneficial owner requires immediate decline before
boarding, or immediate suspension of settlement and escalation to the sanctions officer if the match
arises after boarding. Funds must be held and no payment released pending instruction from the
sanctions officer.

5. Discounting and evidence
A potential match may only be discounted where a secondary identifier — date of birth, national
identifier, registration number or address — conflicts with the listed subject. The discounting
rationale, the identifiers compared, and the identity of the reviewer must be recorded. Name-only
similarity is never sufficient grounds to either confirm or discount a match.

6. Segregation of duties
The analyst who dispositions a potential match may not be the analyst who requested the screening
where the outcome is to discount a match on a critical list. Confirmation of a true sanctions match
requires approval by a second reviewer independent of the first.

7. Politically exposed persons
A politically exposed person in the ownership or control chain is not a decline. It requires
enhanced due diligence, source of wealth evidence, senior management approval and annual review.

8. Prohibited automation
Confirming a sanctions match, discounting a match on a sanctions list, and filing a suspicious
activity report may never be executed by an automated agent without human approval, irrespective of
model confidence or measured agreement rate.
